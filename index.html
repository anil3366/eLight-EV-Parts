About eLight EV Parts

Stage 1 — EV information platform

What is eLight EV Parts?

eLight EV Parts is being developed as an information and catalogue platform for electric vehicle models, spare parts and components.

Stage 1 focuses on organizing EV model and component information in a searchable platform covering two-wheelers, three-wheelers and four-wheelers.

Future stages can introduce seller registration, customer enquiries, verified listings and commercial services.
